Brain size typically shrinks as people age past adulthood, and such shrinkage may be linked to dementia. Therefore, any intervention that can protect against brain shrinkage could help to protect the elderly against dementia and Alzheimer's disease. Researchers in China recently investigated whether different kinds of exercise/activity might help to prevent brain shrinkage or perhaps even lead to an increase in brain size (Mortimer et al., 2012 1 ).
