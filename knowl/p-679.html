We cannot use the bootstrap distribution directly to estimate a p-value because it will be centered at the observed sample statistic rather than at the hypothesized value for the parameter. However, the bootstrap distribution does tell us about the sample-to-sample variation in the statistic. In fact, with quantitative data, we can simply shift the “population distribution” over to center at the hypothesized parameter value without changing the shape or variability of the distribution.
