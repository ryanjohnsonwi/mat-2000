Mathematical symbols can be used to represent quantities and simplify your writing. The Greek letter \(\pi\) (pronounced "pie") is used to represent a parameter that is a long-run proportion, such as the probability that a (future) subject in this study would choose bottled water as most preferred. The symbol \(\hat{p}\) (pronounced "p-hat") is used to represent a statistic that is the proportion of "successes" in a sample, such as the proportion of subjects in this study who did choose bottled water as the most preferred. The symbol \(n\) is used to represent the sample size.
