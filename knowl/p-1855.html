A similar calculation is performed to find the expected cell count for each of the six cells of the table. The chi-square statistic is obtained by squaring the differences in the observed and expected counts, dividing by the expected count (a form of standardizing), and then summing the six values. This can be thought of as \((O - E)^2 / E\text{,}\) where where \(O\) = observed count and \(E\) = expected count.
