Key Idea 3.1.27.

When sampling from a large population, the standard deviation of sample proportions is estimated by the same formula,

\begin{equation*} \text{standard deviation of sample proportions} = \sqrt{\dfrac{\pi(1 - \pi)}{n}}, \end{equation*}

where π represents the proportion of successes in the population and n represents the sample size. The population size is considered large enough when it is more than 20 times that sample size.
