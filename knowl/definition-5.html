Definition 2.2.2.

  • Null hypothesis typically states the "by-random-chance-alone" explanation, which is used to create the chance model, also called the null model. A null hypothesis is expressed symbolically as \(H_0\text{.}\)
  • Alternative hypothesis typically states the "an-effect-exists" explanation that contradicts the null hypothesis. An alternative hypothesis is expressed symbolically as \(H_a\) .
