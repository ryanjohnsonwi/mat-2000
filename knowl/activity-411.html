Exercise 8.2.1.

You will first randomize the order (jumping jacks first or bicycle kicks first) in which you measure your heart rate. If heads:
  • Do jumping jacks for 30 seconds. Then, measure your jumping jack heart rate.
  • Sit down and take a break for two minutes.
  • Now do bicycle kicks for 30 seconds. Then, measure your bicycle kick heart rate.
If tails:
  • Now do bicycle kicks for 30 seconds. Then, measure your bicycle kick heart rate.
  • Sit down and take a break for two minutes.
  • Do jumping jacks for 30 seconds. Then, measure your jumping jack heart rate.
(a)
Report your heart rate for bicycle kicks and jumping jacks in beats per minute.
(b)
Find the difference in your two heart rates (jumping jack heart rate-bicycle kick heart rate) in beats per minute.
Your instructor will give you and your classmates instructions on where to record your jumping jack heart rate, bicycle kick heart rate, and difference (jumping jacks-bicycle kick).
