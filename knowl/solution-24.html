Looking back, it would be interesting to test different human cues other than bowing (eg. pointing, nodding, etc.). Also it would be interesting to test these same cues using a non-human robot to see if non-human cues are understood as well as human cues. Finally, it would also be a good idea to test many different breeds of dogs as there may be differences between breeds in their understanding of human and non-human cues. Looking forward, it may be that dogs respond better to verbal cues rather than gestations.
