Notice one new aspect here compared to earlier: When we are sampling from a finite population rather than from an infinite random process, we want the population to be large in order to assume π represents a constant probability of success. In this case, \(268 > 200( = 20 \times 10)\text{,}\) and the formula should apply. In which case, we can use the same theory-based methods we used before.
