  • \(\pi\) is the underlying process probability of success or population proportion of success.

  • \(n\) is the sample size.

  • \(\hat{p} = \frac{\text{number of successes in the sample}}{n}\) is the sample proportion.

  • \(SD = \sqrt{ \pi(1-\pi) / n } \) is the theory-based standard deviation of the null distribution.

  • \(\text{standardized statistic} = \frac{\text{statistic} - \text{mean of the null distribution}}{\text{SD of the null distribution}} \) is the number of standard deviations away from the mean of the sampling distribution a statistic is.

  • \(z = \frac{\hat{p} - \pi}{\text{Simulated SD of null distribution}} \) is the simulation-based standardized statistic for one proportion.

  • \(z = \frac{\hat{p} - \pi}{SD} = \frac{\hat{p} - \pi}{\sqrt{ \pi(1-\pi) / n } } \) is the theory-based standardized statistic for one proportion.
