Validity Conditions 10.2.32. for the ANOVA F-test.

The F-distribution is a good approximation to the null distribution of the F-statistic as long as:

  • Either the sample size is at least 20 for all the groups, without strong skewness or outliers; or if the sample size is less than 20, then the distribution of the response variable is approximately symmetric in all the samples (examine the dotplots for skewness or outliers).

  • The standard deviations of the samples are approximately equal to each other (largest standard deviation is not more than twice the value of the smallest standard deviation).
