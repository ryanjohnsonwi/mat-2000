Researchers wondered whether different socially normed messages led to different rates of towel reuse in hotels. Rooms were randomly assigned to have one of 5 socially-normed signs hung on the towel bar. Data were collected on 1595 instances of potential towel reuse. It was hypothesized that there would be an association between type of message and towel reuse. Data gathered showed 37.0% towel reuse in the no normed sign group, 44.1% reuse in the guest-normed group, 49.3% in the room-normed group, 43.4% in the citizen-normed group, and 41.0% in the gender-normed group.
