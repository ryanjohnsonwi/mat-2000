\((\overline{x}_1 - \overline{x}_2) \pm \text{(multiplier)} \times (SE) \) is a theory-based confidence interval estimating \(\mu_1 - \mu_2\text{,}\) where the multiplier comes from a t distribution with \(\frac{ \big( \frac{s_1^2}{n_1} + \frac{s_2^2}{n_2} \big)^2 }{ \frac{1}{n_1-1}\big( \frac{s_1^2}{n_1} \big)^2 + \frac{1}{n_2-1}\big( \frac{s_2^2}{n_2} \big)^2 } \) degrees of freedom.
