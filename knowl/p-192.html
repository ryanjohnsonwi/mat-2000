A second factor that influences the strength of evidence against the null is how far apart the observed sample statistic and the value of the parameter specified under the null hypothesis are. For this study the null value was 0.50 and the observed sample statistic was about 0.543 (or 54.3% of the competitors wearing red won their matches). Suppose a larger proportion of competitors wearing red won their matches. In fact, suppose 57% of the 457 matches were won by a competitor wearing red.
