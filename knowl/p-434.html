Your simulation analysis should convince you that the sample data provide very strong evidence to believe that kissing couples lean their heads to the right more than half the time in the long run. That leads to a natural follow-up question: How much more than half the time? In other words, we have strong evidence that the long-run proportion of kissing couples who lean to the right is greater than 1/2, but can we now estimate the value for that probability? We will do this by testing many different (null) values for the probability that a couple leans to the right when kissing.
