F-statistic (ANOVA test):
  • \(\mu_i\) are the population means for the groups.
  • \(n_i\) are the sample sizes for the groups with \(n\) as the overall sample size.
  • \(\overline{x}_i\) are the sample means for the groups.
  • \(s_i\) are the sample standard deviations for the groups.
  • \(l\) is the number of treatments (groups).
  • \(df\) are the degrees of freedom for the F-distribution.
  • \(SS\) is the sum of squares.
  • \(MS\) is the mean squares.
  • \(F\) is calculated as the ratio \(F = \frac{\text{between-group variability}}{\text{within-group variability}} = \frac{MS_{Treatment}}{MS_{Error}} \text{.}\)
  • \(R^2\) is the "coefficient of determination" the proportion of the variation in the response variable which is explained by the treatment, calculated as \(R^2 = \frac{SS_{Treatment}}{SS_{Error}} \text{.}\)
  • The mean of an F-distribution is \(\frac{df_{Error} }{ df_{Error} - 2} \text{.}\)
  • The SD of an F-distribution is \(\sqrt{ \frac{2 (df_{Error})^2 (df_{Treatment} + df_{Error} - 2 )}{df_{Treatment} (df_{Error} -2)^2 (df_{Error} - 4)} } \text{.}\)
  • \(\displaystyle df_{Treatment} = l - 1 \)
  • \(\displaystyle df_{Error} = n - l \)
  • \(\displaystyle df_{Total} = df_{Treatment} + df_{Error} = n -1 \)
  • \(\displaystyle SS_{Treatment} = \sum n_i(\bar{x}_i - \bar{x})^2 \)
  • \(\displaystyle SS_{Error} = \sum (n_i-1)(s_i^2) \)
  • \(\displaystyle SS_{Total} = SS_{Treatment} + SS_{Error} \)
  • \(\displaystyle MS_{Treatment} = \frac{SS_{Treatment}}{df_{Treatment}} = \frac{\sum n_i(\bar{x}_i - \bar{x})^2}{l-1} \)
  • \(MS_{Error} = \frac{SS_{Error}}{df_{Error}} = \frac{\sum (n_i-1)(s_i^2)}{n-l} \text{.}\)
