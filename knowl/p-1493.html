Now you will obtain numerical summaries (statistics, such as mean and SD) of the survey data using the Multiple Means applet 3 .
  • Open the data file CloseFriends 4  to access the raw data. Copy the data (e.g., CTRL-A and CTRL-C).
  • Open the Multiple Means applet 5  and press Clear. Click inside the Sample data box and paste (e.g., CTRL-V). Then press Use Data.
