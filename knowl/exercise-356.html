Exercise 15.

Analyze these data to address the question of whether American men and women differ with regard to donating blood. Include a descriptive analysis (graphs and numbers) as well as an inferential analysis (p-value and confidence interval). Use theory-based approaches, if you decide that the validity conditions are satisfied, for the inferential analysis. If you decide that men and women differ significantly, be sure to estimate by how much they differ. Write a paragraph or two summarizing your analysis and conclusions.
