Tire study

A legendary story on college campuses concerns two students who missed an exam because of excessive partying, but they blamed the absence on a flat tire. The professor allowed the students to take a make-up exam in separate rooms. On the exam, the students were asked which tire was flat.

Step 1: Ask a research question.

Do students pick which tire went flat in equal proportions? A common conjecture is that students tend to answer "front right" more often than would be expected by chance.

Step 2: Design a study and collect data.

To test the conjecture, a professor asked 28 students to imagine being in the same situation as the students in the story and pick one of the four tires. The data is summarized in the table below.
