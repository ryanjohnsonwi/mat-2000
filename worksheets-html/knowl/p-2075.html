Now we’ll fill in the SS (SSE) column, the Sum Squared Error. For the first number, the Model row, compute the sum of four Squared Error (SE) numbers from the last exercise. For the last number, the Total row, compute \(s^2 \times n\text{,}\) in other words, the standard deviation of the entire sample times the sample size of the whole sample. For the middle entry, corresponding to the Error row, compute \(\text{SS}_{\text{Total}}-\text{SS}_{\text{Model}}\text{,}\) in other words, the last entry of this column minus the first entry.
