The "placebo effect" has been found in numerous studies: When subjects are told something good is going to happen, they often have a positive response even if nothing is actually done to them. For this reason, subjects are often kept "blind" as to which treatment group they are placed in, for example by giving one group the actual treatment and the other group a "fake" treatment, like a placebo (an inert pill). Placebo treatments have even been used in studies about knee surgery!
