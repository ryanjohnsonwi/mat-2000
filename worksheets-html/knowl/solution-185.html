Randomly picking 10 words and finding the mean of these words to be 6 letters or more is the least likely because as the sample size increases from 1 to 5 to 10 the standard deviation of the sampling distribution will be progressively smaller and a value of 6 will become further and further in the tail of the distribution as the sample means cluster more closely around 4.295.
