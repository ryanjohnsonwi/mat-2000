Paragraph

  • Data are the values measured or categories recorded on individual objects of interest.
  • The observational unit is the individual object on which data are recorded.
  • A variable is a characteristic being recorded about the observational units.
    • A quantitative variable has numeric values for which ordinary arithmetic (addition, multiplication) makes sense.
    • A categorical variable places observational units into categories.
  • A distribution is the pattern of outcomes of a variable.
