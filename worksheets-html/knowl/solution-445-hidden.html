We have very strong evidence of a difference in average prices paid using the two different auction formats because our p-value was < 0.0001.
We are 95% confident that the price of the card in the Dutch auction being, on average, between $0.24 and $0.53 higher than the price in the first-price sealed bid auction.
Cause-and-effect is possible here because cards were randomly assigned to treatment groups (type of auction) so we have convincing evidence that the type of auction causes the difference in average prices.
We cannot generalize this result beyond cards similar to those in the study--- there is no guarantee that these 88 pairs of magic cards are somehow representative of “all” magic cards.
