Now we’ll fill in the MS (MSE) column, the mean squared error column. For the Model and the Error columns, take the SS (SSE) number to the left divided by the df number further left, i.e. \(\text{MS} = \frac{\text{SS}}{\text{df}}\text{.}\) Leave the bottom cell empty because there is no mean squared error for the Total row.
