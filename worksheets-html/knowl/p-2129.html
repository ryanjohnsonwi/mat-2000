Mean Group Diff:
  • \(\mu_i\) are the population means for the groups.
  • \(n_i\) are the sample sizes for the groups.
  • \(\overline{x}_i\) are the sample means for the groups.
  • \(s_i\) are the sample standard deviations for the groups.
  • The Mean Group Diff is the mean of the absolute value of the differences of sample means, and can be computed directly as \(\displaystyle \frac{\sum_{\text{groups } i,j} |\overline{x}_i - \overline{x}_j | }{ (l-1)(l/2) }, \) where \(l \) is the number of groups.
