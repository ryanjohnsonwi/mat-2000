It turns out that a more common criterion for determining the “best” line is to instead look at the sum of the squared residuals (SSE). This approach is similar to simply adding up the residuals but is even more strict in not letting individual residuals get too large. Check the Show Squared Residuals box to visually represent the squared residual for each observation. Note that we can visually represent the squared residual as the area of a square where each side of the square has length equal to the residual.
