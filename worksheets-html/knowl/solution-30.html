Example answer:
9/10 is more unusual than 6/10 because 5/10 is what happens “on average” when Harley is just guessing--- an outcome farther away from the typical outcome is less likely. Since the cup bowed to (or pointed to) in each trial was randomly chosen and each of the two studies was conducted the same with the exception of who/what did the bowing/pointing, there is evidence the difference in the proportions correct between the studies was caused by the difference in human vs mechanical pointing. These two analyses suggest that Harley seems to understand human cues better than non-human cues---further studies in a variety of situations with Harley (different kinds of human cues, different non-human cues, etc.) and with other dogs of different breeds are now needed to pursue this research area further
