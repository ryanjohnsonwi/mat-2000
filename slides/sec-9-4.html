Remark 6:

Key Idea . You may be thinking.

Earlier we subtracted proportions, and now we are using counts. Can we not use proportions anymore?

Sure, you can still work with proportions. If you used proportions, your MAD statistic would subtract the observed proportions from the hypothesized proportions (in this case, 1/7 or 0.143 for each day) for each day of the week and then average the absolute values of those differences.

Revisiting step 4: Draw inferences

Theory-based Chi-square Goodness-of-fit Test

Earlier, for Step 4, you used simulation to find a p-value using the chi-square statistic. Recall that one advantage of using the chi-square statistic (instead of the MAD) is that the distribution of the chi-square statistic can be predicted by a theory-based distribution called the chi-square distribution.
