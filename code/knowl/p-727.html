The key to our simulation analysis is to assume that if yawning is not contagious (null hypothesis), then the 14 yawners would have yawned regardless of whether they had seen the yawn seed. Similarly, we'll assume that the 36 nonyawners would not have yawned, no matter which group they had been assigned. In other words, our simulation assumes the null hypothesis is true—that there is no association, no connection, between the yawn seed and actual yawning.
