Key Idea 3.2.21.

When taking random samples from a large population (more than 20 times the size of the sample), the distribution of sample means will

  1. Center around the population mean (\(\mu\))

  2. Have a standard deviation smaller than the population, \(SD(\bar{X})=\dfrac{\text{population standard deviation}}{\sqrt{\text{sample size}}}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}\)

  3. Be approximately normal when the sample size is large (by the Central Limit Theorem or if the population distribution is normally distributed)
