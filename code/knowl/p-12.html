  • \(x_i \) is the value of the \(i\)th observation for the variable of interest.

  • \(n\) is the sample size.

  • \(\overline{x} = \frac{\text{sum of observations in the sample}}{n} = \frac{\sum x_i }{n} = \frac{x_1 + \ldots + x_n }{n} \) is the sample mean, which is a measure of center for a sample. The sample mean is sometimes called the average.

  • \(\displaystyle s = \sqrt{ \frac{\sum (x_i - \overline{x})^2 }{n-1} } = \sqrt{ \frac{ (x_1 - \overline{x})^2 + \ldots + (x_n - \overline{x})^2 }{n-1} } \) is the sample standard deviation, which is a measure of the typical deviation of the data values from the mean for a sample.
