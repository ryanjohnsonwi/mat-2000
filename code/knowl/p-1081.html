Recall that researchers asked volunteer subjects to imagine they moved to a new state, applied for a driver's license, and needed to decide whether to become an organ donor. Researchers created three different versions of the survey, each with a different donor recruiting strategy. Subjects were randomly assigned to one of the three types of recruiting strategies:

  • The default option is to be an organ donor, and individuals have to opt out of organ donation (opt-out group)

  • The default option is to not be an organ donor, and individuals have to opt in to organ donation (opt-in group)

  • There is no default option, and individuals have to choose whether they will be an organ donor (neutral group)
