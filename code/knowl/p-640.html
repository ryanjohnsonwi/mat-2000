The diagram in the figure below illustrates the preceding key idea. The top panel shows the possible confounding effect if the study design had not been randomized. We want to know whether differences in the explanatory variable cause differences in the response (dotted arrow). However, a confounding variable would affect both the explanatory and response variables, making it impossible to separate the effects of explanatory and confounding variables on the response. The bottom panel shows how randomization removes the effect of the potential confounders.
