Although the Mean Group Diff statistic is fairly easy to understand and calculate, it is not commonly used, in part because there is no theory-based model for the null distribution. Another downside is that the Mean Group Diff statistic is not standardized so it is not comparable across studies (e.g., a Mean Group Diff statistic of 1 in one study might be strong evidence but in another study might not show convincing evidence against the null hypothesis).
